How it works

From the real thing to a clean print.

  1. 01

    Scan it

    Walk around the object with an iPhone or iPad that has a LiDAR Scanner. The app guides you round every side.

  2. 02

    Add close-ups

    A scan gets the shape right but softens small details. The app tells you which close-ups to take, like a port or a seam.

  3. 03

    Clean it up

    Your companion fills holes, smooths out lumps and checks the size. Your close-ups put the fine details back.

  4. 04

    Print it

    The clean model goes to your printer server, which slices it for your printer. Nothing prints until you say so.

Printing

Prints go through tools makers already use.

  • bambu-printer-mcp

    For Bambu Lab printers. It slices with your printer’s exact settings, matches AMS filament and checks the print file before it’s sent.

    bambu-printer-mcp on GitHub

  • mcp-3D-printer-server

    For OctoPrint, Klipper, Duet, Repetier, Prusa, Creality and Bambu Lab printers.

    mcp-3D-printer-server on GitHub

  • Change it first

    Both servers work with Blender, so an AI agent can change the model before it prints: scale it, give it a wider base, or cut a slot.

Devices

Made for iPhone, iPhone Duo and iPad.

Scanning needs a LiDAR Scanner. Everything else works on any iPhone or iPad that runs iOS 27 or iPadOS 27.

  • Scanning needs LiDAR

    Scan to Make uses Apple’s Object Capture with the LiDAR Scanner, which is on Pro models of iPhone and iPad. On a device without one, the app tells you plainly instead of guessing the size from photos.

  • iPhone Duo, folded or open

    Layouts are made for both ways of holding it. iPhone Duo has no LiDAR Scanner, so it can’t scan, but it can open a scan made on another device, add close-ups and send prints.

  • iPad, on the big screen

    Every screen has its own iPad layout, and iPad Pro models with LiDAR can scan.

  • iOS 27 or later

    Scan to Make needs iOS 27 or iPadOS 27, or later.

Your computer, your network

Clean-up runs on a computer you own.

Your iPhone or iPad scans, and you make the choices. The companion, on your own computer, cleans up the scan and passes it to your printer server, over your own private network.

With your own companion, scans and photos go from your device to it, not to us. Clean-up that uses an AI model sends your close-ups and views of the scan to that model’s provider.

Questions

Good to know.

Do I need a 3D printer?

Yes. Prints go to your own printer through bambu-printer-mcp, for Bambu Lab printers, or mcp-3D-printer-server, for OctoPrint, Klipper, Duet, Repetier, Prusa, Creality and Bambu Lab.

Which iPhones and iPads can scan?

Ones with a LiDAR Scanner, which Apple puts in Pro models, running iOS 27 or iPadOS 27 or later. Other iPhones and iPads, including iPhone Duo, run the rest of the app.

Why not scan with the camera alone?

LiDAR measures depth directly, so the scan comes out at real size. Without it the app would be guessing, so it tells you instead.

What does clean-up fix?

Holes, lumps and rough patches the scan picked up, plus a size check against measurements you know. Close-ups help it put back details like ports and seams.

What is the companion?

Software that runs on a computer you own, on your own network. It does the clean-up and hands the result to your printer server.

Is it open source?

Yes. Scan to Make is built so you can run all of it yourself, and its source will be public before release. The printer servers already are.

We’ll share pricing before launch.
