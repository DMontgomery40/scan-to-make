Release notes and checksums

Mac, with iPhone and iPad

  1. Move Counterpart to Applications and open it. Allow Local Network access so it can reach your phone and printer.
  2. In Settings › Printers, add your printer. Counterpart installs the printer software it needs.
  3. For design and changes, choose your AI in Settings › AI: Claude Code, Codex, an API key, or a local model.
  4. Install Counterpart on your iPhone or iPad from TestFlight and open it. With the same Apple Account it finds your Mac by itself; otherwise choose Add a Phone in the Mac's menu bar and scan the code.
  5. For print progress on your Lock Screen, turn on Live Activity updates in the Mac's Settings › Advanced.

Linux

  1. Open the .deb to install Counterpart, or make the AppImage executable and open it.
  2. Open Counterpart and let it finish setting up.
  3. In Settings › Printers, add your printer. Install the slicer your printer software uses, such as Bambu Studio.
  4. For design and changes, choose your AI in Settings.
  5. To pair an Android phone, show the code in Settings.

Android

  1. Download the app on your phone and open it. Allow your browser to install it when Android asks.
  2. Make sure Counterpart is open on your Mac or Linux computer, on the same Wi-Fi as the phone.
  3. Open Counterpart, choose Continue, and scan the code the computer shows, or choose Look on This Wi-Fi and check the six-digit code matches.

Scan cleanup on the Mac

Scan cleanup uses Blender and a few free tools that install separately. Open Counterpart once, then follow the cleanup tools guide. Printing published models doesn't need them.

Start with these

The first time you open Counterpart, it offers two starter projects. Tap one to open a project with the designer's instructions, then download their original file from MakerWorld and add it to the project.

  • 3D scan platform by Kass 3D: six plates and a quick assembly, for steady scans of small objects.
  • Long vernier caliper by 3D Print Masters Hub: two colours, with an optional depth gauge.
