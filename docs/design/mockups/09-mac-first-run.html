Mac · First run and Settings

Set up once, in four short steps

Open the app and one small window walks you through it: pick your printer and how it prints, choose how AI helps (or skip it), then pair your iPhone with a QR code. Blender downloads quietly in the corner while you answer, so there’s no Terminal and no waiting screen. Everything chosen here lives in Settings afterwards, with network addresses and version numbers kept under Advanced.
