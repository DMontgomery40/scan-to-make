Scan to Make

Point your iPhone at a thing. Your Mac cleans it up, gets the size right and talks to your printer. You tap Print, check the photo of the bed, and walk away. These mockups show every step, on iPhone, iPhone Duo, iPad and the Mac.

Scan something→Name it→Close-ups (optional)→Mac cleans it up→Print→The bed is clear — Start print→Take it off the bed
