Scan to Make for Mac: a cube in the menu bar

Everything after the scan runs on the Mac, so the Mac app keeps out of the way. It is one icon and one popover that shows what's happening now, your last five projects and your printer. People who like to tinker get one-click doors into Blender, Bambu Studio and Finder. Whatever they change there comes back to the same project.

Working, and a phone asks to pair. The cube gets a clay dot while the Mac cleans up, and the popover shows what's running (with the printer's camera and Stop), the last five projects and the printer. Hovering a project shows its doors: Open in Blender, Open in Bambu Studio, Show in Finder, and ••• for the rest. A new iPhone asking to pair gets a card at the top of the popover that stays until you answer, so it works with notifications off; the macOS notification says the same thing, with the same code the phone shows. All projects… opens a searchable list of every project, with the same doors.
