**Rules for whoever leads** (David, Nov 9: "it's kind of shocking to see the lack of discipline and tracking and crack-sealing so things don't fall through cracks ... not to mention the ideas that pop up along the way"):
- Anything David says that is a defect, a wish or an idea gets an issue (or a comment on the matching one) in the same turn it is said, in his words, before any work starts on it.

- PRs say `Refs #N`, never `Fixes`/`Closes`: an issue closes only as **done** (the PR and the evidence from David's real install) or **dropped** (who said so, quoted).
- Every open issue has an owner in its body or latest comment: a thread link, "Lead", or "David" for what only he can do. The lead keeps #610 current at every wake.
- Times come from the clock (`date -u`), never written ahead of it.
